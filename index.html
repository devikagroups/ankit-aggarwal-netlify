About

A legacy carried forward, one plan at a time.

Ankit Aggarwal is the Managing Director of Devika Group, stepping into active leadership in 2007 from the group's corporate headquarters at Devika Tower 6, Nehru Place, New Delhi. He represents the next generation of a real-estate house whose roots reach back to 1954.

Across nearly two decades in the industry, his remit spans strategic planning, project execution and expansion — carrying the founding emphasis on trust and disciplined delivery into organized commercial space, high-street retail, hospitality and mixed-use development across Delhi NCR, Uttar Pradesh and Goa.

Under his direction, the group has continued strengthening its footing in established markets such as Nehru Place and Sadar Bazar while opening new ground in emerging corridors, balancing decades of experience with a contemporary approach to urban planning.
